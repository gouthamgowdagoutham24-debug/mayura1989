About Mayura 1989

A legacy of Andhra
flavours since 1989.

Born in 1989, Mayura has grown into a destination for authentic Andhra flavours and memorable dining experiences in Bengaluru.

Rooted in bold, vibrant recipes, aromatic spices and traditional hospitality, every dish carries the warmth of a table made for togetherness. From signature specialities and biryanis to tandoor favourites and contemporary selections, Mayura is a celebration of flavour, tradition and memorable meals.
